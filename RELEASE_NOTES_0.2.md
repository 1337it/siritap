## Siritap 0.2

The first public release. Use an Apple TV Siri Remote as a trackpad, scroll wheel, media remote and voice keyboard for your Mac.

**Download:** `Siritap-0.2.dmg` below. Open it, drag Siritap into Applications, open it and allow Accessibility. Signed and notarized by Apple. macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.

### Highlights
- Pointer control, tap zones with edge arrows, press-to-click and drag.
- Edge scrolling with momentum, and ring scrolling on the 2nd-generation remote that starts the moment you touch the ring.
- Double-tap and tap-then-swipe gestures.
- Remap every button: clicks, shortcuts, media keys, brightness, Mission Control, desktops, shell commands.
- Per-app actions, with VLC set up out of the box.
- Voice typing: hold Siri, speak, let go. Uses the Mac's microphone with any remote, or the 2nd-generation remote's own microphone. No Xcode, PacketLogger or profile needed.
- Voice commands such as "press enter", "new line" and "undo", plus your own phrases.
- Separate settings for each remote, including voice.

### Known limits
- 1st-generation remotes can't send their own microphone audio to a Mac, so they use the Mac's microphone for voice.
- Only one app can drive the remote at a time: quit Remote Buddy or SiriMote first.
